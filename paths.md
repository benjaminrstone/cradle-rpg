# Paths

Techniques follow the shared rules and scaling in techniques.md. Numbers are for a typical fighter in units of their own stage (Channel cap 15). Damage is shown at 6 / 10 / 15 madra unless noted.

Every fighter can also strike, dodge, block, and move, whatever their Path.

## Path of Black Flame

Fire and destruction madra. Black Flame hits harder for its madra than anything else, and spends fast. The Path has no defensive techniques: its users defend by being fast.

**Element property:** 1.3× damage efficiency. Black Flame burns its user too: every 5 madra spent on Black Flame (upkeep included) costs 1 health, and every 10 costs 1 spirit. Damage reduction doesn't apply.

| Technique | Type | Madra | Wind-up | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dragon's Breath | Striker | Any, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 4 | A beam of black fire. 11 / 20 / 33 damage. +1 to hit. Any range; best at Mid |
| Burning Cloak | Enforcer stance | 6 to raise | 1 | Speed ×1.5 and +5 physical damage. Upkeep 5 madra per tick |
| Void Dragon's Dance | Ruler | Any amount: no channel cap | Madra ÷ 2 | A finisher: a long build-up, then one blast of black fire over a wide area. As a Ruler technique, you keep acting while it builds. Area attack, no roll; hits everything at Close and Mid range. 21 / 43 / 64 damage at 12 / 20 / 30 madra (wind-ups of about 5, 8.5 and 13 ticks). Boosted by fire and destruction in the area: +1 damage for every point of Black Flame madra spent while it winds up |

**Learning the Path:** Dragon's Breath, Burning Cloak and Void Dragon's Dance are core (free).

**How you win:** Burning Cloak makes you faster than your opponent, so you hit more often, dodge better, and act more often. Dragon's Breath does more damage per madra than any other Striker technique, so a few clean hits end the fight. Void Dragon's Dance is the finisher: build it up, keep it from being interrupted, and feed it with the rest of the Path until it lands.

**How you lose:** the madra runs out, and the flame wears you down while it does. Burning Cloak drains the pool and your body in a long fight, and with no Guard techniques you can't stall. An opponent who stays evasive and makes you chase loses less than you spend.

## Path of the Endless Sword

Sword madra. Sword aura cuts, so it slips past toughness, and the Path's signature technique fills the space around you with slashes, erupting from every blade nearby.

**Element property:** sharpness. Ignores 3 points of physical damage reduction (a typical fighter's whole Toughness).

| Technique | Type | Madra | Wind-up | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Endless Sword | Striker / Ruler | Any, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 3 | Sword aura slashes everything within close and mid range, including constructs. 6 / 11 / 19 damage to each target. Area attack: no roll. Each target is hit once more for every other blade close to it: an opponent's weapon, a Forged sword, a blade on the ground. Your own weapon doesn't count; it's what makes the others resonate |
| Rippling Sword | Striker | Any, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 4 | A strike whose edge extends into a wave of sword aura. 10 + madra × efficiency: 18 / 25 / 36. Any range; best at Close or Mid (−1 to hit at Far) |
| Flowing Sword | Enforcer stance | 4 to raise | 1 | Builds up while held, with no limit: every 5 ticks, +10% Speed and +1 sharpness. Upkeep starts at 1 madra per 2 ticks and rises by 0.1 madra per tick with each build-up, so the pool is what eventually stops it. A hit of twice your interrupt threshold breaks it, like any stance: it drops and all build-up is lost |

**Learning the Path:** Endless Sword, Rippling Sword and Flowing Sword are core (free).

**How you win:** sharpness cuts through Toughness, so tough fighters lose their edge. Flowing Sword makes you faster and sharper the longer the fight goes, as long as you aren't hit hard. Endless Sword is an area attack, so even the fastest opponent can't dodge it, and every extra blade on the field adds another hit: armed opponents, sword constructs, and blades left lying around.

**How you lose:** no Guard techniques. A fighter who stays at long range, or who is fast enough to dodge anyway, makes you pay full price for every swing. One solid hit breaks Flowing Sword, so an opponent who lands early keeps you from ever building up.

## Path of the Hollow King

Pure madra, built for taking techniques apart: it strips stances and constructs, punches through madra defenses, and throws attacks back at their owners. Low raw damage, and its defenses cost upkeep.

**Element property:** pure madra. No elemental resistance applies to it, and madra defenses are half as effective against it: blocking it, or reinforcing a construct against it, costs double madra, and it does double damage to madra constructs (a barrier soaks it, but loses 2 HP per point soaked). This applies to everything the Path does with madra, not to physical strikes.

| Technique | Type | Madra | Wind-up | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow King's Spear | Striker | Any, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 4 | Hyper-compressed pure madra. Spiritual, 0.85× damage: 7 / 13 / 22 before reduction. +1 to hit. Any range; best at Mid |
| Hollow Armor | Enforcer stance | 5 to raise | 1 | Halves technique and spiritual damage; no help against physical hits. Upkeep 1 madra per tick |
| Crown of the Hollow King | Forger | Any per star, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 2 per star | Two uses. **Forge a star:** the madra you put in sets the star's size; it forms high overhead at Far range after madra ÷ 2 ticks, but your next turn comes after 1 tick, so you keep acting while it forms. Each star is its own construct with HP equal to its madra; only attacks that reach Far can touch it. **Drop stars:** a quick action that drops any number of forged stars, biggest first. They land 0.5 ticks later as one attack with a hit per star, and your next turn comes then. The defender reacts once, and blocking them all costs the multi-hit rate. Each hit is rolled, spiritual, 2 × the star's madra before reduction, +1 to hit, at any range; spiritual hits count double against the threshold, so a decent star breaks wind-ups |
| Hollow King's Mantle | Striker stance | 5 to raise | 1 | While it is up, you can catch an incoming non-area technique and throw it back at its owner for half the madra that went into it. It lands on them 1 tick later. Doesn't work on physical hits or Crown stars. Upkeep 1 madra per tick |
| Madra Pulse | Striker / Ruler | 2 or more, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 6 | A wash of pure madra at Close range only. It cancels each of your opponent's wind-ups, stances and constructs there that cost at most half the madra put in: a wind-up by the madra in it, a stance by its raise cost, a construct by its madra (a barrier's is half its HP). The opponent can block it, at double cost for pure madra: each point of block takes 1.5 off its strength. No damage, no roll |

**Learning the Path:** Hollow King's Spear, Hollow Armor and the Crown are core (free). The Mantle costs 1 point; Madra Pulse costs 2.

**How you win:** dismantle the opponent's plan. Madra Pulse wipes out their cheap stances, constructs and wind-ups, and makes them pay double to save the rest; the Spear hits spirit and is costly to block; the Mantle turns their biggest technique against them; and stars forged in the quiet moments drop all at once to break whatever they're winding up and grind down their spirit.

**How you lose:** low raw damage, and Hollow Armor and the Mantle cost 1 madra per tick each, so holding both drains the pool fast. Physical fighters ignore both, and area attacks can't be redirected.

## Path of the Dawn Oath

Force madra and Oathsign script circles. It holds ground behind barriers and an anchored stance, shuts down the opponent's techniques with seals, and closes distance in a blink for heavy hammer blows.

**Element property:** force madra. Ziel fights with a hammer (+4 physical damage).

| Technique | Type | Madra | Wind-up | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stone Anchor | Enforcer stance | 5 to raise | 1 | +6 damage reduction against physical and technique hits, and your stances take 50% more to break (24 at Control 5). Upkeep 0.5 madra per tick |
| Oathsign: Force Ring | Enforcer | Any, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 3 | Script rings multiply a hammer blow: 10 + weapon + madra × efficiency, physical: 22 / 29 / 40 with the hammer. Close range only |
| Oathsign: Barrier | Forger | Any, up to channel cap | Madra ÷ 3 | A script-circle shield with HP of 2 × madra, filling in as it builds while you keep acting. It soaks physical, technique and piercing hits aimed at you before they reach you; other spiritual hits pass through. Casting again adds to it |
| Oathsign: Launch | Forger | 4 | 0.5 travel | A ring flings you one range band, in or out, in 0.5 ticks; your next turn comes 1 tick after you land |
| Oathsign: Seal | Forger | 10 | 2 | A script circle Forged on your opponent. Rolled disruption: if it hits, it breaks all their wind-ups and stances. Any range; best at Mid |

**Learning the Path:** Stone Anchor, Force Ring and the barrier are core (free). Launch costs 1 point; Seal costs 2.

**How you win:** be hard to hurt and hard to break. Stone Anchor and a barrier soak what gets through, Seal strips the opponent's stances and wind-ups from any range, and Launch puts you in hammer range before they can step away.

**How you lose:** no damage beyond Close range. Spiritual attacks pass through the barrier and ignore Stone Anchor, and Madra Pulse disperses everything at once.
