# Traits

Traits come from outside a fighter's Path: Goldsigns, iron bodies, bloodlines. Like techniques, they're hidden until revealed or scouted.

## Goldsigns

### Sword arms

Blades that grow from the back.

Grows with stage, letting you make more kinds of attack twice in a single action:

| Stage | Can be made twice at once |
| --- | --- |
| Lowgold | Strikes |
| Highgold | Empowered strikes |
| Truegold | Enforcer strike techniques, like Rippling Sword |

- Each strike rolls to hit separately.
- Two madra-powered strikes share one channel cap, so two half-strength strikes cost the same as one full one.
- **The arms count as one blade** for anything that cares, such as Endless Sword.
- **Cost when building a fighter:** 3 points.

## Weapons

A weapon adds its damage to strikes and to weapon techniques. A blade also counts as an extra blade for Endless Sword when its wielder is the target.

When building a fighter, a weapon costs 1 point per +2 damage, rounded up. Being a blade is free.

| Weapon | Carried by | Damage | Blade | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sword | Yerin | +0 | Yes | 0 |
| Scissors | Eithan | +0 | Yes | 0 |
| Hammer | Ziel | +4 | No | 2 |
