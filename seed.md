# Seed: a Cradle fighting game

A tempo game where every power costs time or madra. Cut anything that doesn't put a visible decision or a visible counter in front of the player; anything hidden gets a loud reveal when it's used. The clock is the game. Madra is the budget. Techniques are the content.

## The game

A fighting game set in Cradle, playable both PvP and PvE. Hidden information is part of the fun in both.

Building a fighter is a big part of the game: lots of room for choice, flavor, and a fighter that feels unique. That trades off against ease of use and understanding (the PF1 vs 5E tradeoff).

## The loop

Fighters share one initiative queue. When you reach the top, you act: pick a technique, commit madra, and its wind-up goes on the queue where everyone can see it. Different actions push you different distances down the queue. Anyone can react out of turn, but reacting pushes their own next action later.

Everything that takes time lives on the queue: wind-ups, upkeep on held techniques, cooldowns, and Forger constructs acting on their own.

Fights should be short enough that a single powerful action matters, long enough that a budget runs out.

## The triangle

The clock creates counterplay on its own if power costs time. Three ways to fight, each beating one and losing to one:

- **Press** (fast, cheap strikes) interrupts **Build**.
- **Build** (slow, heavy techniques) overwhelms **Guard**.
- **Guard** (toughness, held stances) shrugs off **Press**.

Techniques should generally trade off between time, power, and madra efficiency. Techniques with unique or secondary effects should be carefully balanced.

## Hits

Attacks land based on a combination of skill and luck, represented by an attack roll with modifiers. Keep the spread small. Defenses are static or active, both are constants rather than rolls.

Many defenses work as flat damage reduction, which gives Guard its shape for free: small hits get soaked, big hits get through. Particulars depend on the technique.

A hit above a threshold interrupts a wind-up. Spiritual hits have a lower threshold. Madra already channeled into an interrupted technique is lost; madra not yet put in isn't.

## Two resources, two defenses

Time and madra. Evade with time: a dodge delays your next action. Absorb with madra: a block or stance spends budget. Nimble fighters bleed tempo, tough fighters bleed pool.

The channel cap is the most madra you can push through one action, helps prevent fights from becoming single actions using the entire madra pool.

Held techniques cost upkeep while they're up, so drop them the moment they've done their job. A dropped defense has a delay before it can be raised again.

Madra regenerates slowly in a fight. Running dry isn't losing, but it leaves you depending on physical dodging and fighting.

Soulfire joins as a third resource at higher stages.

## Health and spirit

Two tracks, and running out of either loses the fight. Spirit is separate from the madra pool. Most fights are won on health. Spirit damage is the specialist's route: it can cause a temporary loss of control, disrupt techniques, and destroy madra constructs.

## Range and movement

The final design has a battlemap. Prototype with range bands. Movement and movement techniques are important aspects of the fight.

## Legibility

- Madra can be sensed, so wind-ups are public. The type of technique is visible, the effects of it usually not until it has been used (or scouted).
- Anything unknown gets a loud, clear reveal the first time it's used: a technique, an iron body, a bloodline trait.
- Counter-traits (like an iron body that can't be interrupted) are rare and hidden until revealed or scouted. That's part of what makes scouting valuable, and what keeps powerful opponents fresh.
- The defender sees the attack before choosing how to react (let it miss, take the hit, dodge out of the way, block or deflect or reflect), along with the chance to hit for each option. The roll happens after they choose how to react.

## Statistics

Core statistics should represent (solely or in combination) stage, skill, speed, health, toughness, spirit, spiritual defense, madra pool, and channel cap. The exact list of statistics is not yet defined.

## Techniques

Four basic types of techniques. Later stage techniques blur the lines and may contain elements of multiple types. Most fighters have some techniques of each type; someone with only one type is very specialized.

Each type has its own way to win and way to lose.

| Type | Role in a fight | How you win | How you lose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enforcer | Empowering your body, a weapon, or a single action. Stances with ongoing boosts. Movement | Make each of your actions more efficient than theirs: harder hits, a guard that soaks more, faster movement | A burst breaks your stance and wastes the upkeep you paid, or the opponent won't fight on your terms (staying at range, waiting out your upkeep) |
| Striker | Range and pressure | Force the opponent to spend time and madra dodging and blocking until their defenses give out | They close the distance, are tough enough to shrug off your hits, defend more cheaply than you attack, or set up their own win condition before you do enough damage |
| Forger | Persistent madra constructs that have effects or take actions until destroyed | Constructs cost madra and time up front, then snowball if left alone | The opponent ignores them and takes you out first, destroys them cheaply (they're weak to spiritual damage), out-builds you, or lands one big technique while you Forge small ones |
| Ruler | Terrain and aura. Big changes take a long wind-up; small nudges using Soulfire (a push of air) are fast | Reshape the field so every later action favors you. A slow investment with a big payoff | You're interrupted during the wind-up, the fight ends before the payoff, or the opponent leaves the area you've shaped |

Techniques have element(s), madra costs, speed, and effects. Elements (types of madra: fire, earth, shadow, sword, pure, etc) interact with terrain and aura and resistances. Rare elements may have unique effects.

## Stage gaps

Stage multiplies speed and other stats, but not skill. A wall is right for the fiction, so don't try to make a duel across it fair. A lower stage fighter might use terrain, numbers, or preparation to make up some of the gap.

## Inspiration - Eithan vs Sha Miara
Eithan kicked off, Enforcing his body. While the Monarch’s voice still echoed, Eithan launched a punch at Miara’s face. Nine-colored eyes tracked him easily, and her hand pushed his aside. In the same motion, she spun into a technique with her other hand. The heel of Eithan’s left hand caught her in the chin, sending a pulse of pure madra through her head. She was lifted off her feet in a blow that would have rattled anyone else, but her technique was not interrupted. The Moonheart Iron body. Developed in a country neighboring Ninecloud, it had been co-opted by the royal family to make up for some of their weaknesses. Not only did it toughen the body and allow for impressive physical feats, its main feature was that trauma to the body did not affect mental or spiritual control whatsoever. It was more than just resisting pain; a high-grade Moonheart Iron body separated the consciousness from the flesh. She could fight as though controlling herself at a distance. Eithan knew that, so he gave her no space. Before her body flew away from the force of his first blow, he grabbed the collar of her robe and prepared the Hollow Armor. Her technique, a net of rainbow light, landed on him. Her Absolute Decree was meant to sink through his skin and restrict his spirit, subjecting him to her control, but it landed on a layer of pure madra around him instead. He dropped his own technique the instant hers landed, instead of wasting madra to leave it up. The Absolute Decree was disrupted but not broken, but it had been weakened enough that his inherent spiritual defenses destroyed it. That created an opening as Miara tried and failed to control him. In that second, he swept her legs out from under her. He tried to slam her into the ground, but she spun out of his grip, using the aura control of her soulfire to push against the air and gain unnatural leverage. He had already begun Forging stars of madra over her head. The Crown of the Hollow King. She raised one hand to take them over, and that was the mistake that gave him the victory. There was one reliable way to defeat an opponent with superior raw abilities: skill. Sha Miara was too focused on her power to take over his techniques. There had been an instant in which she could have seized the lead in the fight instead of staying on the defensive. She’d missed it. He wouldn’t give her another chance. Now he was one move ahead. While she was taking over his floating crown of blue-white stars, he used his Striker technique: the Hollow Spear. At higher levels of advancement, this technique would show its true power. As it was, it was just a hyper-compressed beam of pure madra designed to stab a hole through madra-based defenses. But he knew what she would do against the Spear, so he started another technique. The Hollow King’s Mantle. Madra swirled around his body in a vortex. She conjured the Nine-Light Mirror, a circle of royal madra bigger than her body. His spear sunk into it and was reflected, blasting back at him with an added rainbow light that showed her power had been added to his. The Nine-Light Mirror caught techniques and returned them stronger…but he had a similar power of his own. The whirlpool of madra around him, the Hollow King’s Mantle, caught his returned spear. It whipped the Striker technique around his body in an orbit and flung it at Sha Miara. To avoid wasting madra and cutting off her own view, Miara dropped the Mirror as soon as she could. Once she did, there was a delay before she could activate it again. He had expected that. Before the fight had even started. And he’d timed his Mantle accordingly. The Hollow Spear pierced her chest the instant the Nine-Light Mirror vanished. The Spear might inflict some spiritual damage, but at this level it was better at breaking techniques than enemies. At best, it would disrupt any Enforcer techniques she had forming. Now he was two moves ahead. He closed the gap an instant behind the Spear, Forging more stars for the Hollow King’s Crown overhead. This time, he infused soulfire into the technique, so the stars formed larger and brighter. The next move he used wasn’t technically part of the Path of the Hollow King, but he thought it was appropriate under the circumstances. He lowered himself to slam an Empty Palm into Sha Miara’s core. The restricted Monarch might as well have been a training dummy. She was so far on the defensive that she reacted only along straight, predictable lines. She leaped back to try to create distance while moving to block his palm strike and hurling rainbow madra up into the sky. Too easy to read. He had already aimed for where she was going. His makeshift Empty Palm landed. Her royal madra dispersed, and the Crown of the Hollow King finished Forging. Even with the Moonheart Iron body, her eyes went wide. She could still read and react to the battle with perfect clarity, but her spirit had fallen briefly out of her control. Five stars from the Crown speared down, driving through her madra channels and severing her spirit. It was the most savage spiritual attack of which he was capable, and ninety-nine out of one hundred sacred artists would be killed or permanently disabled. But he had to assume Celestial Radiance sacred artists had superior spiritual defenses. Which was where his remaining two attacks came into play. A Hollow Spear suffused with soulfire pierced her lower abdomen as black scissors flashed into his hand. He couldn’t safely activate the Archlord binding within, but he didn’t need to. The weapon was a perfect conductor for madra and soulfire. He Enforced the weapon and drove it through her temple. No sooner had he pierced the skin than she dissolved into white light.
